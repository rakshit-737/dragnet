## R - per-report ATT&CK cases (group x cited report)

Labels are ATT&CK's own attribution. **k-fold**: 5 folds by report; each fold's reports are removed from the profiles before its cases are attributed. **Temporal**: every citation dated 2022 or later is removed from the profiles, and the 219 cases from reports dated 2022 or later are attributed. Top-1 CIs are clustered by group.

Citation dating: of 1947 citations on 'uses' edges, 1852 are dated from their reference description '(YYYY, Month DD)', 12 only from a year in the citation key, and 83 have no recoverable date ('n.d.'). 52 citations whose key has no year are dated 2022+ by their description and held out (an earlier version dated by key only and kept them in the profiles); 26 keys carry a year that differs from the description. Held out: 547 citations (630 when undated citations are also held out, the sensitivity rows below).

## G - comparison with Guru, Moss & Kochenderfer (2025)

Guru et al. (arXiv:2505.11547) attribute threat reports to 29 actors by scoring P(technique | actor), estimated from technique counts in training reports, against the techniques extracted from a test report (machine extraction with text-embedding-3-large over 727 reports); the metric is the mean rank of the true actor among the 29 (random = 15). Paper protocol: 70/20/10 split per actor; 10 weight matrices are trained, the best is selected on the validation split and its test mean rank is reported; the paper does not define its +/-. Their report corpus and extracted technique counts are not public in reusable form, so the paper's numbers cannot be reproduced exactly. Our adaptation keeps their scorer, actor set and 70/20/10 split per actor, but evaluates every one of 10 random splits on its test part (the 20% validation part is unused) and uses ATT&CK's human-curated per-report technique lists (261 reports). Human technique lists are cleaner than machine extraction, so the adaptation is an optimistic upper bound for their pipeline. DRAGNET and the other methods use graphs from which every validation/test report has been removed.
